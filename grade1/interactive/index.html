🎮 Grade 1 — Interactive

On-screen games & explorers — tap, drag, and listen. Best on a tablet or computer.

🧮 Math — 9 activities
⚡ Ten-Frame Flash Peek at a ten-frame for a moment, then tell how many dots you saw. 👆 Count & Tap Tap each picture to count it, then pick how many there are (up to 20). 🐸 Number Line Hopper Find missing numbers counting by 1s forward and back, and skip counting by 2s, 5s, 10s. 🃏 Make 10 Pairs Flip cards to find pairs that make 10 (or 20) in a memory-match game. 🏎️ Math Facts Race Answer as many + and − facts as you can in 60 seconds. Beat your best! 🧱 Build It with Blocks Build numbers to 99 with tens rods and ones cubes. 🔟 Regroup with Ten-Frames Move counters to fill a ten-frame and use make-a-ten to add, like 8 + 5. 🐊 Hungry Alligator Compare numbers and sums with <, = and > — the alligator eats the bigger one! 👂 Listen & Solve Hear short story problems, see the pictures, and add or subtract within 20.
📖 Reading — 10 activities
👂 Listen & Pick Hear a word, tap the one you heard: short vowels, long vowels, digraphs & blends. 🔤 Missing Vowel Look at the picture and tap the vowel that finishes the word (c_t). 🧱 Word Builder Drag letter tiles into the boxes to spell the picture word: CVC, blends, silent e. 🎢 Sound Slider Slide through the sounds slowly, then fast, and pick the matching picture. ⚡ Sight Word Flash Cards Flash cards for sight word sets 1–3. Missed words come back until you know them all. 🎵 Rhyme Time Which word rhymes? Listen and pick the rhyming word from three pictures. 🏠 Word Family Sort Drag words into their family houses: -at, -an, -ig, -op, -ug, -ell and more. 🧩 Sentence Scramble Put the mixed-up words in order. Capital first, period last, then hear it read. 📖 Read-Along Stories Six short decodable stories read aloud with word highlighting, plus 3 questions each. 💥 Compound Word Smash Smash two words into one (sun + flower = sunflower), or split a compound word apart.

🔈 Many activities read words and questions aloud using your browser’s built-in voice — turn your sound on! These activities are for the screen and are not printable; for printable practice go back to Grade 1. ⭐ Stars you earn are saved on this device.
