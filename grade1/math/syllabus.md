# Grade 1 Math — Comprehensive Syllabus

A complete year-long math syllabus aimed at a Canadian Grade 1 level (ages 6–7). It covers every strand expected at this level — number sense, operations, patterning & algebra, measurement, geometry, data, and financial literacy — whether or not practice sheets currently exist in the repo. Links to existing repo sheets are included as 📂 references; missing materials are marked **(needs exercises)** so they can be filled in later.

The school year is divided into four terms. Pacing is flexible — slow down, repeat, or skip as needed.

---

## Strands and Big Ideas

Grade 1 math is organized around six strands:

1. **Number Sense** — counting, place value, comparing, representing numbers.
2. **Operations** — addition, subtraction, and the relationship between them.
3. **Patterning & Algebra** — repeating and growing patterns, equality.
4. **Measurement** — length, mass, capacity, area, time.
5. **Geometry & Spatial Sense** — 2-D shapes, 3-D objects, position, symmetry.
6. **Data, Probability & Financial Literacy** — sorting, graphing, chance, money.

Each term cycles through several strands so concepts spiral rather than appear once and disappear.

## Term 1 — Numbers to 50 & Foundations (September – November)

### 1. Counting
- Count forward by 1s from any number up to 50 (later 100).
- Count backward by 1s from 20, then 50.
- Skip count forward by 2s, 5s, and 10s to 50, then 100.
- Skip count backward by 2s, 5s, 10s.
- Count on from a given number ("start at 17 and count 4 more").
- Use a hundreds chart and number line.

📂 **Repo materials:** [Counting — index](01_counting/index.html) — [Counting Forward](01_counting/01_counting_forward.html), [Counting Backward](01_counting/02_counting_backward.html), [Skip Counting Forward](01_counting/03_skip_counting_forward.html), [Skip Counting Backward](01_counting/04_skip_counting_backward.html).

### 2. Representing Numbers to 50
- Match numerals, number words, and quantities.
- Represent numbers with: objects, fingers, tally marks, ten frames, base-10 blocks, number lines, hundreds chart.
- Write numerals correctly (formation). **(needs exercises)**

### 3. Comparing & Ordering Numbers to 50
- Compare two numbers: more / less / equal.
- Use the symbols `<`, `>`, `=`.
- Order three or more numbers from least to greatest and greatest to least.
- Find one more, one less, ten more, ten less. **(needs exercises)**

### 4. Composing & Decomposing Numbers to 10
- Show all the ways to make each number 2–10 (e.g. 7 = 0+7, 1+6, 2+5, 3+4).
- Use ten frames, fingers, and number bonds.
- Recognize "doubles" (1+1, 2+2, … 5+5) and "doubles plus one". **(needs exercises)**

### 5. Patterning & Algebra (light intro)
- Identify, extend, and create simple repeating patterns with objects, sounds, and shapes (AB, ABB, ABC, AAB).
- Describe the pattern rule in your own words. **(needs exercises)**

### 6. Geometry (light intro)
- Name and describe 2-D shapes: circle, square, triangle, rectangle, oval, hexagon.
- Sort shapes by attribute (number of sides, corners, curved vs. straight). **(needs exercises)**

## Term 2 — Addition & Subtraction Within 20 (December – February)

### 1. Number Sense to 100
- Continue counting, comparing, and ordering to 100.
- Read and write number words to 20.
- Locate numbers on a hundreds chart and number line. **(needs exercises)**

### 2. Addition Within 10
- Join, combine, and add-to situations.
- Strategies: counting all, counting on, using fingers, ten frames, drawings.
- Memorize addition facts to 10.

📂 **Repo materials:** [Addition Up to 10](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/01_addition_up_to_10.html).

### 3. Subtraction Within 10
- Take-away, comparison, and missing-part situations.
- Strategies: cross-out drawings, counting back, counting up, ten frames.
- Memorize subtraction facts to 10.

📂 **Repo materials:** [Subtraction Up to 10](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/03_subtraction_up_to_10.html).

### 4. Addition & Subtraction Within 20
- **Make-a-ten** strategy for addition (8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3 = 13).
- **Subtract-through-10** strategy (13 − 5 = 13 − 3 − 2 = 8).
- Doubles and near-doubles for facts to 20.
- Use vertical and horizontal formats.

📂 **Repo materials:** [Addition Up to 20](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/02_addition_up_to_20.html), [Subtraction Up to 20](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/04_subtraction_up_to_20.html), [Mixed Add & Subtract Up to 10](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/05_mixed_add_sub_up_to_10.html), [Mixed Add & Subtract Up to 20](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/06_mixed_add_sub_up_to_20.html).

### 5. Relationship Between Addition & Subtraction
- Fact families (e.g. 3, 4, 7 → 3+4=7, 4+3=7, 7−3=4, 7−4=3).
- Use addition to solve subtraction (think-addition strategy).
- Find missing addends: `5 + ☐ = 9`. **(needs exercises for missing addends)**

### 6. Word Problems
- Solve one-step add/subtract word problems with sums and minuends to 20.
- Use pictures, equations, and oral explanations.
- Identify the operation needed from the story context.

📂 **Repo materials:** [Word Problems — Add & Subtract to 20](01_addition_subtraction_up_to_20/07_word_problems.html).

### 7. Equality & the Equal Sign
- Understand `=` as "is the same as", not "the answer is".
- Decide if equations are true or false (e.g. 6 + 2 = 8, 3 + 4 = 7 + 0).
- Balance both sides: `5 + 3 = ☐ + 4`. **(needs exercises)**

### 8. Measurement — Length
- Compare lengths and heights directly (longer / shorter / same; taller / shorter).
- Order three or more objects by length.
- Measure with non-standard units (cubes, paper clips, hand spans). **(needs exercises)**

## Term 3 — Place Value & Two-Digit Addition (March – May)

### 1. Place Value to 100
- Group objects into tens and ones.
- Read, write, and represent two-digit numbers as tens + ones (47 = 4 tens + 7 ones).
- Show numbers with base-10 blocks, ten-frames, and place-value tables.
- Expanded form: 47 = 40 + 7. **(needs exercises for explicit place-value drills)**

### 2. Comparing Numbers to 100
- Compare two-digit numbers using tens then ones.
- Use `<`, `>`, `=` with two-digit numbers.
- Find 10 more / 10 less mentally. **(needs exercises)**

### 3. Two-Digit Addition Without Regrouping
- Add a one-digit number to a two-digit number (e.g. 23 + 5).
- Add two two-digit numbers when no regrouping is needed (e.g. 23 + 14).
- Use horizontal, vertical, place-value table, and ten-frame formats.
- Solve word problems with two-digit no-regrouping addition.

📂 **Repo materials:** [Addition Without Regrouping — index](02_addition_no_regrouping/index.html) — [Tutorial](02_addition_no_regrouping/00_no_regrouping_tutorial.html), [Up to 20](02_addition_no_regrouping/01_no_regrouping_up_to_20.html), [Up to 30](02_addition_no_regrouping/02_no_regrouping_up_to_30.html), [Up to 40](02_addition_no_regrouping/03_no_regrouping_up_to_40.html), [Up to 50](02_addition_no_regrouping/04_no_regrouping_up_to_50.html), [Up to 100](02_addition_no_regrouping/05_no_regrouping_up_to_100.html), [Ten-Frame Exercises](02_addition_no_regrouping/06_ten_frame_exercises.html).

### 4. Two-Digit Addition With Regrouping
- Understand that 10 ones can be regrouped (carried) as 1 ten.
- Add two-digit numbers with regrouping, starting within 20, then 50, then 100.
- Use ten-frames, base-10 blocks, place-value tables, and vertical carrying.

📂 **Repo materials (within 20):** [Addition With Regrouping — index](03_addition_with_regrouping/index.html) — [Tutorial](03_addition_with_regrouping/01_regrouping_tutorial.html), [Regrouping Exercises — Up to 20](03_addition_with_regrouping/02_regrouping_exercises_up_to_20.html), [Ten-Frame Regrouping Exercises](03_addition_with_regrouping/03_ten_frame_exercises.html).

Extensions **(needs exercises):** two-digit + two-digit with regrouping up to 50 and up to 100; two-digit subtraction with and without regrouping.

### 5. Patterns & Algebra
- Identify and extend repeating, growing, and shrinking patterns (numerical and with shapes).
- Find missing numbers in a sequence (skip-count patterns).
- Begin function-machine thinking: "in → +3 → out". **(needs exercises)**

### 6. Measurement — Mass, Capacity, Area, Time
- **Mass:** compare and order objects as heavier / lighter using a balance.
- **Capacity:** compare and order containers as holds more / holds less.
- **Area:** compare two surfaces using non-standard units (tiles).
- **Time:**
  - Days of the week, months of the year, seasons.
  - Tell time to the hour and half hour on analog and digital clocks.
  - Use vocabulary: yesterday, today, tomorrow, morning, afternoon, evening, night. **(needs exercises)**

### 7. Geometry & Spatial Sense
- Compose and decompose 2-D shapes (two triangles can make a square).
- Recognize 3-D objects: cube, sphere, cylinder, cone, rectangular prism, pyramid.
- Describe 3-D objects by faces, edges, and corners (informally).
- Position words: above, below, beside, between, in front of, behind, left, right.
- Identify lines of symmetry in simple shapes. **(needs exercises)**

## Term 4 — Application & Year-End Strands (June)

### 1. Fractions — Equal Shares
- Partition shapes into 2 and 4 equal shares.
- Use the words **halves**, **fourths**, and **quarters**.
- Understand that more equal shares of the same whole means each share is smaller. **(needs exercises)**

### 2. Data Management
- Sort objects by one or two attributes.
- Pose simple data questions and collect responses (favourite fruit, pet, colour).
- Organize data with concrete graphs, pictographs, and tally charts.
- Read and answer questions about a graph: most, least, same, total. **(needs exercises)**

### 3. Probability (informal)
- Use everyday language to describe chance: always, sometimes, never, more likely, less likely, equally likely. **(needs exercises)**

### 4. Financial Literacy — Money
- Recognize and name Canadian coins: nickel (5¢), dime (10¢), quarter (25¢), loonie ($1), toonie ($2), and the $5 bill.
- Know the value of each coin.
- Make simple values with combinations of coins (e.g. show 15¢, 30¢).
- Recognize that goods and services cost money. **(needs exercises)**

### 5. Review & Mixed Practice
- Mixed-strategy practice: addition and subtraction within 20, two-digit addition with and without regrouping, word problems.
- Cumulative review of measurement, geometry, data, and money. **(needs cumulative-review exercises)**

## End-of-Year Goals

By the end of Grade 1, the learner should be able to:

**Number Sense**
- Count, read, write, compare, and order numbers to 100.
- Skip count by 2s, 5s, and 10s to 100, forward and backward.
- Understand two-digit numbers as tens + ones.

**Operations**
- Recall addition and subtraction facts within 10 fluently and use efficient strategies within 20.
- Add two-digit numbers without regrouping (sums to 100) and with regrouping (introductory).
- Solve one-step word problems and explain the strategy used.
- Understand `=` as "is the same as" and find missing addends.

**Patterning & Algebra**
- Identify, extend, and create repeating and simple growing patterns.

**Measurement**
- Compare and order objects by length, mass, capacity, and area using non-standard units.
- Tell time to the hour and half hour; name days, months, and seasons.

**Geometry**
- Name and describe common 2-D shapes and 3-D objects.
- Compose, decompose, and identify symmetry in simple shapes.
- Use position words accurately.

**Data, Probability & Money**
- Sort, graph, and interpret simple data.
- Use everyday chance language.
- Identify Canadian coins and make simple combinations.

**Fractions**
- Partition shapes into halves and fourths and use the matching language.
