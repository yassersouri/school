# Grade 1 — Year-Long Syllabus

A year-long Grade 1 syllabus aimed at a Canadian Grade 1 level, covering **learning to read** and **math**. Where possible, each topic links to the matching printable practice sheets already in this repository.

The school year is split into four terms. Pacing is flexible — repeat or skip sections as needed.

---

## Term 1 — Strong Foundations (September – November)

### 📖 Reading
- Review the alphabet: letter names, letter sounds, and letter formation.
- Hear and say the sounds at the beginning, middle, and end of short words.
- Blend and segment simple CVC words with **short vowels**.
- Begin a personal word bank of common sight words (the, and, is, a, I, to, see, my, like, go, can …).
- Read short predictable sentences using known sounds and sight words.

**Practice sheets from this repo:**
- [Short Vowels — index](reading/01_short_vowels/index.html) — 22 CVC word-family sheets covering short **a**, **e**, **i**, **o**, **u**.
  - Start with the single-vowel introductions: [Short A](reading/01_short_vowels/01_short_a.html), [Short E](reading/01_short_vowels/02_short_e.html), [Short I](reading/01_short_vowels/03_short_i.html), [Short O](reading/01_short_vowels/04_short_o.html), [Short U](reading/01_short_vowels/05_short_u.html).
  - Move into specific word families: -it/-id, -ip/-ick, -ill/-im, -ap/-ad, -ag/-am, -ack/-ash, -et/-ell, -eck/-est, -og/-ock, -ob/-ox, -ut/-ub, -uck/-ump.
  - Finish each vowel with the **Mixed Practice** sheets (⭐ sheets 18–22) before moving on.

### 🧮 Math
- Count forward and backward to 20, then 50, working toward 100 by the end of the term.
- Skip count by 2s, 5s, and 10s with objects, fingers, and a number chart.
- Read, write, compare, and order numbers to 50.
- Represent numbers with objects, fingers, ten frames, tally marks, and number lines.
- Compose and decompose numbers to 10 (e.g. 7 = 5 + 2 = 4 + 3).

**Practice sheets from this repo:**

  - [Skip Counting Forward](math/01_counting/03_skip_counting_forward.html) (by 2s, 5s, 10s)

## Term 2 — Growing Fluency (December – February)

### 📖 Reading
- Build automaticity with short-vowel word families — revisit any [Short Vowel Mixed Practice](reading/01_short_vowels/index.html) sheets that still feel tricky.
- Introduce **long vowels** using silent-e (CVCe) and common vowel teams.
- Notice the difference between short and long vowel sounds in similar words (cap / cape, kit / kite).
- Continue growing the sight-word bank; add Grade 1 high-frequency words (was, are, you, they, said, have, what, when, with …).
- Read short decodable books and re-read for fluency.

### 🧮 Math
- Add and subtract within 10 using counters, drawings, fingers, and number lines.
- Extend to addition and subtraction within 20 using the **make-a-ten** and **subtract-through-10** strategies.
- Recognize **fact families** and the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- Solve simple one-step word problems and explain the strategy used.

## Term 3 — Applying Skills (March – May)

### 📖 Reading
- Explore common word endings that change meaning: practice the **adding -y** pattern (turn a noun into a describing word).
- Learn the **double-the-letter** rule (sun → sunny, mud → muddy) vs. **just-add-y** (rain → rainy, dust → dusty).
- Begin reading **short stories** with comprehension questions — answer who, what, where, when, why, and how.
- Use decoding, sight words, context, and pictures to figure out new words.
- Read aloud with smoother pacing, accuracy, and simple expression.

### 🧮 Math
- Understand **place value**: tens and ones for numbers to 100.
- Add two-digit numbers **without regrouping** using place value, ten-frames, and base-10 thinking.
- Begin adding two-digit numbers **with regrouping** (carrying): start with the visual tutorial, then practice up to 20.
- Solve word problems using pictures, equations, and oral explanations.

### Additional Grade 1 math topics (no repo materials yet)
These appear in a typical Canadian Grade 1 program. They are listed here so they aren't forgotten — practice materials can be added later.
- **Measurement:** compare lengths, heights, masses, and capacities using non-standard units.
- **Geometry:** name and describe 2-D shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle) and 3-D objects (cube, sphere, cylinder, cone).
- **Time:** tell time to the hour and half hour on digital and analog clocks; days, weeks, months, seasons.
- **Money:** identify Canadian coins (nickel, dime, quarter, loonie, toonie) and make simple combinations.
- **Data:** sort objects, build simple graphs (concrete, pictograph, tally), and answer questions about the data.
- **Fractions:** equal shares — halves and quarters of a whole.

## Term 4 — Review and Independence (June)

### 📖 Reading
- Review phonics patterns learned during the year (short vowels, long vowels, -y endings).
- Re-read favourite [Sentences & Stories](reading/04_sentences/index.html) for fluency, expression, and comprehension.
- Read familiar and new Grade 1 texts with growing independence.
- Summarize, retell, and share an opinion about books read.
- Build daily reading stamina through short, joyful independent reading.

### 🧮 Math
- Review the year: counting and skip counting, place value, addition and subtraction within 20, and two-digit addition with and without regrouping.
- Choose efficient strategies for addition and subtraction problems.
- Solve mixed word problems using pictures, equations, and oral explanations.
- Bring in the additional Grade 1 topics (measurement, geometry, time, money, data, fractions) through hands-on activities.

## End-of-Year Goals

By the end of Grade 1, the learner should be able to:

**Reading**
- Decode regular short-vowel and long-vowel (CVCe / vowel-team) words.
- Read common Grade 1 sight words automatically.
- Apply the -y endings rule (double or just add -y) when reading new words.
- Read short stories and answer comprehension questions about them.
- Read aloud with growing accuracy, pacing, and expression.

**Math**
- Count, compare, represent, and work with numbers to 100.
- Add and subtract within 20 fluently using known strategies.
- Add two-digit numbers with and without regrouping.
- Solve simple word problems and explain the strategy used.
- Show beginning understanding of measurement, geometry, time, money, data, and fractions.
