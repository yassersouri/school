🗓️ Grade 1 Year-Long Syllabus

Canadian Grade 1 level — math and learning to read

This syllabus outlines clear, year-long Grade 1 topics for building early number sense and confident reading habits. It is designed for a Canadian Grade 1 learner and can be paced flexibly across the school year.

Term 1 — Strong Foundations

September to November

🧮 Math

  • Count forward and backward to 50, then toward 100.
  • Read, write, compare, and order numbers to 50.
  • Represent numbers with objects, ten frames, tally marks, and number lines.
  • Compose and decompose numbers to 10.
  • Begin addition and subtraction stories within 10.
  • Identify, describe, and extend repeating patterns.

📖 Reading

  • Review letter names, letter sounds, and alphabet order.
  • Blend and segment simple CVC words with short vowels.
  • Build a bank of common sight words and classroom words.
  • Read simple predictable sentences with picture support.
  • Practise left-to-right tracking, one-to-one word matching, and rereading.
  • Retell key details from read-alouds and short texts.

Term 2 — Growing Fluency

December to February

🧮 Math

  • Add and subtract within 20 using drawings, ten frames, and mental strategies.
  • Understand place value as tens and ones for numbers to 100.
  • Skip count by 2s, 5s, and 10s using real objects and number charts.
  • Compare lengths, heights, masses, and capacities with non-standard units.
  • Name and describe 2-D shapes and 3-D objects in the environment.
  • Solve simple one-step word problems and explain the strategy used.

📖 Reading

  • Read short vowel word families with increasing automaticity.
  • Introduce long vowel patterns, including silent-e and common vowel teams.
  • Notice blends, digraphs, and common endings such as -s, -ing, and -ed.
  • Read short decodable books and simple nonfiction texts.
  • Answer who, what, where, when, why, and how questions.
  • Use punctuation to support expression and phrasing.

Term 3 — Applying Skills

March to May

🧮 Math

  • Strengthen addition and subtraction fluency within 20.
  • Add two-digit numbers without regrouping using tens and ones.
  • Explore equal shares, halves, and quarters through concrete materials.
  • Tell time to the hour and half hour on digital and analog clocks.
  • Identify Canadian coins and make simple money combinations.
  • Collect, sort, display, and discuss class data using simple graphs.

📖 Reading

  • Read longer decodable passages and early reader books.
  • Use decoding, sight words, context, and pictures to solve new words.
  • Build vocabulary through stories, poems, songs, and nonfiction topics.
  • Identify characters, setting, problem, solution, main idea, and details.
  • Read aloud with smoother pacing, accuracy, and expression.
  • Make text-to-self, text-to-text, and text-to-world connections.

Term 4 — Review and Independence

June

🧮 Math

  • Review number sense, place value, patterns, measurement, shapes, money, and data.
  • Choose efficient strategies for addition and subtraction problems.
  • Solve mixed word problems using pictures, equations, and oral explanations.
  • Complete hands-on math tasks connected to Canadian daily life and classroom routines.

📖 Reading

  • Review phonics patterns and high-frequency words learned during the year.
  • Read familiar and new Grade 1 texts with growing independence.
  • Summarize, retell, ask questions, and share opinions about books.
  • Build daily reading stamina through short, joyful independent reading.

End-of-Year Goals

  • Students can count, compare, represent, and work with numbers to 100.
  • Students can solve Grade 1 addition and subtraction problems using clear strategies.
  • Students can decode many regular words, recognize common sight words, and read simple texts with understanding.
  • Students can talk about what they read and show confidence as beginning readers and mathematicians.
