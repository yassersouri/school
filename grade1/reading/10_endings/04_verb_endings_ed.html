⏪ Verb Endings: -ed

Add -ed to talk about the past — what already happened

Page 1 of 2

📏 Rule: For most verbs, just add -ed. For short words ending in one vowel + one consonant (like hop), double the last letter, then add -ed.

jump → jumped    hop → hopped
