📏 -er & -est

Use -er to compare two and -est for the most of all

Page 1 of 2

📏 Rule: Add -er to compare two things. Add -est when something is the most of all. Short words ending in one consonant double the letter (big → bigger → biggest).

tall → taller → tallest
