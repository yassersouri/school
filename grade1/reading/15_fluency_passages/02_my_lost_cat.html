1) Read the Passage 📖
Read it three times. Try to read it more smoothly each time.
My Lost Cat

My cat is named Pip. One day, Pip ran out the back door. I could not find her!

I looked under the bed. I looked behind the couch. I looked in the yard. No Pip.

Then I heard a soft "meow" from the big tree. Pip was up high on a branch! Dad helped me get a tall ladder. We brought Pip down safely. I gave her a big hug.
