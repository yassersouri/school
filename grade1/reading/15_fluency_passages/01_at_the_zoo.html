1) Read the Passage 📖
Read it three times — once slow, once faster, once smooth.
At the Zoo

I went to the zoo with my mom. We saw a big lion. The lion has a thick mane. It can roar very loud!

Next, we saw a tall elephant. It has a long trunk and big ears. The elephant drinks water with its trunk.

At the end, we saw a funny monkey. The monkey can swing and jump from tree to tree. I love the zoo!

4) Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the passage. Write your answer on the line.
  • Who went to the zoo with the writer?
  • What does the lion have on its head?
  • How does the elephant drink water?
  • What can the monkey do?
  • How does the writer feel about the zoo?
