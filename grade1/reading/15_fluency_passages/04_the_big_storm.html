1) Read the Passage 📖
Notice the ! and ? marks. Use your voice for each!
The Big Storm

The sky was dark. The wind was loud. "Look at the sky!" said Mom.

"Is it going to rain?" I asked.

Then — BOOM! A loud clap of thunder!

"Run inside, fast!" Mom said.

We ran in and shut the door. The rain came down hard. "Are you scared?" Mom asked.

"No, I am safe with you!" I said. We sat by the window and watched the big storm.

3) Read with Expression 🎭
Practice each line. Match your voice to the punctuation!
  • ! "Look at the sky!" — read it BIG and excited.
  • ? "Is it going to rain?" — make your voice go UP at the end.
  • ! "Run inside, fast!" — read it loud, like Mom is hurrying.
  • ? "Are you scared?" — read it soft and gentle.
  • ! "No, I am safe with you!" — read it brave and proud.

4) Comprehension 🤔
Write your answer on the line.
  • What did Mom see first?
  • What loud sound came next?
  • Where did they run?
  • Was the kid scared? Why or why not?
  • Where did they sit at the end?
