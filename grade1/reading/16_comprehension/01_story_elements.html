Sam and the Kite

Sam went to the park on a windy day. He had a red kite.

Sam ran fast, but his kite got stuck high in a tree!

Sam was sad. He could not reach it.

Then a kind girl named Mia came by. She had a long stick. Mia helped Sam tap the kite down. Sam said, "Thank you, Mia!"

Sam and Mia flew the red kite together.
