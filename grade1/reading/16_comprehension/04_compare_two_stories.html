1) Read Both Stories 📖
Read Story A and Story B. Notice what is the same and what is different.
A · Lily's New Bike

Lily got a new red bike. She rode it to the park.

At the park, the bike chain came off. Lily was sad.

Her dad came and fixed the chain. Lily rode home, smiling.

Now Lily always wears her helmet. The next day, she rode to her friend's house and was extra careful.

B · Tom's New Scooter

Tom got a new blue scooter. He rode it to the park.

At the park, a wheel got stuck. Tom was sad.

His mom helped him fix it. Tom rode home, smiling.

Now Tom always locks his scooter. The next day, he rode to his friend's house and was extra careful too.
