1) What is an Opinion? 🤔
An opinion is what YOU think or feel. It cannot be wrong! Use words like:
I thinkI likeI loveMy favouritethe bestbecause
2) Fact or Opinion? ✅
Write F for fact (true for everyone) or O for opinion (what you think).
  • A dog has four legs.
  • Dogs are the best pets.
  • Apples grow on trees.
  • Apples taste better than bananas.
  • Summer is the most fun season.
  • There are seven days in a week.
