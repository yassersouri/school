📝 Parent note: Tongue twisters build fluency by forcing automatic chunk recognition. Don't worry about perfect pronunciation — the goal is repeating each twister three times, slightly faster each round. Color in a star (or just tick it) each time the child reads a twister cleanly. Aim for 1 round (8 twisters × 3 speeds) per session over several days. Celebrate badges loudly!
