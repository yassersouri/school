🧭 Pick-Your-Path Reader: The Dragon Den

Read a passage. Make a choice. Jump to the next number. YOU tell the story!

Page 1 of 2
📖 How to play:
  1. Read passage #1 out loud. Sound out every long word chunk by chunk.
  2. Pick one of the two choices and jump to that number.
  3. Keep going until you reach 🏆 WIN! or 💥 TRY AGAIN!
  4. If you reach a TRY AGAIN, go back to #1 and pick a different path!

⭐ Reader's Reward ⭐

Read this story 4 times. Pick a different path each time so you reach every ending (#4, #5, #6, #7).
Then tell someone the whole story in your own words!

🤔 Talk About It — Pick a Question

  • Which ending was your favorite? Why?
  • Was tiptoeing brave or scared?
  • What other treasures could be in the cave?
  • Pretend you meet the BOB·CAT. What do you say?
📝 Parent note: Branching stories give kids agency — they choose, they read more. The chunked words (FAN·TAS·TIC, MAG·NET·IC, JACK·POT, RAB·BIT, DRAG·ON, SAND·WICH, PEP·PERS, PUFF·Y, TIP·TOES, FOR·EST) are the sounding-out targets. After one playthrough, encourage your child to re-read passages they skipped — aim for all 7 in a session. Ask the “Talk About It” questions to build comprehension on top of decoding.
