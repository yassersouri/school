🎬 You Are the Voice Actor

Pick a kid in your home. Be a different voice for each pal in the comic. Read it out loud — like a real show. Make Dan boom! Make Rab-bit hop! Make Drag-on grin!

📝 Parent note: Comic strips give chunked words a story to live in. The supporting text uses only CVC and common sight words so the child can read the whole panel — not just the targets. Bonus panels A–D are short stand-alone scenes for extra reading. Encourage character voices on the 3rd re-read; fun voicing is shown to boost prosody and comprehension.
