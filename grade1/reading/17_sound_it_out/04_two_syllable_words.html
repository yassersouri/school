Two Chunks, One Big Word 🧱
A long word looks scary — until you break it into chunks you already know.
Try with: sun·set
  • 1
    Spot the chunks (one vowel sound each).
  • 2
    Sound out each chunk:
    /sun/ · /set/
  • 3
    Push them together:
    sunset!

3) Split It Yourself ✂️
Draw a line between the two chunks (between the two vowel parts), then sound it out and write the word on the line.
  • basket
    bas / ket
  • kitten
    kit / ten
  • muffin
    muf / fin
  • mitten
    mit / ten
  • goblin
    gob / lin
  • bandit
    ban / dit
4) Sound Out and Match 🔍
Sound out each word, then write the matching letter (A–F).
  • napkinA. a small animal with long ears
  • picnicB. a hat that keeps your head safe
  • magnetC. a paper square for wiping your mouth
  • rabbitD. a small round thing on a shirt
  • helmetE. an outdoor lunch on a blanket
  • buttonF. a thing that sticks to metal
