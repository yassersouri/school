How to Sound Out a New Word 🐌
When you see a word you don't know, don't worry — sound it out, letter by letter!
Try with: l o g
  • 1
    Look at each letter.
  • 2
    Say each sound:
    /l/ /o/ /g/
  • 3
    Slide them together:
    llll-oooo-ggg
  • 4
    Say the word:
    log!
