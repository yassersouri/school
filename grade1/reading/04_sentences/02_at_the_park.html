1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
A Day at the Park

Sam and Pam went to the park.

Sam ran up the big hill. Pam sat on the green grass.

They saw a red kite in the sky.

Pam had a snack. Sam had a sip of his drink.

It was a sunny, happy day at the park.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • Where did Sam and Pam go?
  • Who ran up the hill?
  • What color was the kite?
  • What did Pam do on the grass?
  • How was the day?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
