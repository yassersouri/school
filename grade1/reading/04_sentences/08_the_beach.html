1) Read the Story 📖

Day at the Beach

Pam and Sam went to the beach.

The sand was hot. The sea was blue.

Sam made a sand cake. Pam got a shell.

They ran in the waves. They had so much fun.

It was a sunny, sandy day.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • Where did Pam and Sam go?
  • How was the sand?
  • What did Sam make?
  • What did Pam get?
  • How was the day?
