1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
My Pup Max

I have a pup. His name is Max.

Max is big and funny. He can run and jump.

Max likes to dig in the mud.

On a sunny day, Max naps on the rug.

I am lucky to have Max.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • What is the pup's name?
  • Is Max big or small?
  • Where does Max like to dig?
  • What does Max do on a sunny day?
  • Who feels lucky?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • Max is a cat. TF
  • Max can run and jump. TF
  • Max likes to dig in the mud. TF
  • Max naps on a hill. TF
  • Max is big and funny. TF
