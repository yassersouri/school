1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
Game Day

It is game day! I am on a team.

We have a red ball. The ball is big.

I run fast. I kick the ball. I jump high.

The team had so much fun on the sunny day.

We had a lucky win!

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • What day is it?
  • What color is the ball?
  • What do I do with the ball?
  • How was the day?
  • Did the team win?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • It is game day. TF
  • The ball is blue. TF
  • I run fast. TF
