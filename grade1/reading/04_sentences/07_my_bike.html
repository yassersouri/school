1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
My Red Bike

I have a red bike. It is fast.

I ride my bike up the hill.

Then I ride down. It is so much fun!

On a sunny day, I ride to the lake.

My bike is the best.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • What color is my bike?
  • Is the bike fast or slow?
  • Where do I ride up?
  • Where do I ride on a sunny day?
  • How do I feel about my bike?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • My bike is red. TF
  • My bike is slow. TF
  • I ride up the hill. TF
  • I ride to the lake on a rainy day. TF
  • I think my bike is the best. TF
