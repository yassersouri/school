1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
My Toy Box

I have a big toy box. It is in my room.

I have a top and a truck. I have a kite, too.

My truck is red. My kite is blue.

I like to play with my toys on the rug.

I am lucky to have so much fun.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • Where is the toy box?
  • What color is the truck?
  • What color is the kite?
  • Where do I play with my toys?
  • How do I feel?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • The toy box is big. TF
  • The truck is blue. TF
  • The kite is blue. TF
  • I play with my toys on the bed. TF
  • I feel lucky. TF
