1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
My Snack

I am so hungry! It is snack time.

Mom gave me jam on a bun.

I had a sip of milk in a cup.

I had a red plum, too. The plum was so good.

Now I am full. Yum!

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • What time is it?
  • What did Mom give me?
  • What was in my cup?
  • What color was the plum?
  • How am I at the end?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • I am hungry at snack time. TF
  • Mom gave me a hot dog. TF
  • I had milk in a cup. TF
  • The plum was green. TF
  • I am full at the end. TF
