1) Read the Story 📖

A Day at School

Ben got up. He had to go to school.

He had a red bag. In it was a pen and a snack.

At school, Ben sat at his desk.

He had to spell ten words. Ben did his best.

It was a fun day at school.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔

  • Where did Ben have to go?
  • What color was his bag?
  • What was in the bag?
  • How many words did he spell?
  • How was the day?
