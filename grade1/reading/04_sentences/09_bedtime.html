1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
Time for Bed

It is late. The sun is not up. I see the moon.

I get in my bed. My bed is soft.

Mom reads me a tale. The tale is about a frog.

I am sleepy. I shut my eyes.

Good night!

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • What do I see in the sky?
  • How is my bed?
  • Who reads to me?
  • What is the tale about?
  • How do I feel?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • The sun is up. TF
  • I see the moon. TF
  • My bed is soft. TF
  • The tale is about a pig. TF
  • I am sleepy. TF
