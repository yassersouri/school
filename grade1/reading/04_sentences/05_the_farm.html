On the Farm

Tim went to a big farm.

He saw a fat pig in the mud.

He saw a red hen on a nest.

A goat sat on a rock. A duck ran in the sun.

Tim had a fun day on the muddy farm.

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • Where did Tim go?
  • Who was in the mud?
  • Where was the hen?
  • Where did the goat sit?
  • How was the farm?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • Tim went to the lake. TF
  • The pig was in the mud. TF
  • The hen was on a nest. TF
