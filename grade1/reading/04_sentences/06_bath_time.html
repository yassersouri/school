1) Read the Story 📖
Read it out loud. Then read it again!
Time for a Bath

I am muddy! It is time for a bath.

I jump in the tub. The tub is full of suds.

I have a duck and a cup in the tub.

I get my hair wet. I get my legs wet, too.

Now I am not muddy. I am clean!

2) Reading Comprehension 🤔
Look back at the story. Write your answer on the line.
  • Why do I need a bath?
  • Where do I jump?
  • What is in the tub with me?
  • What did I get wet?
  • How am I at the end?
3) True or False? ✅ ❌
Read each sentence. Circle T for true or F for false.
  • I am muddy. TF
  • I jump in a pond. TF
  • I have a duck in the tub. TF
  • I get my hair wet. TF
  • I am still muddy at the end. TF
