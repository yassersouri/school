4) One Word at a Time 👉
When we read, one spoken word matches one printed word. Point to each word as you say it. Count the words and write the number.
  • 1. I see a dog. → words
  • 2. The big red bus. → words
  • 3. My mom can run. → words
  • 4. We like to play in the sun. → words
  • 5. Cats and dogs are pets. → words
5) Top to Bottom, Page to Page ⬇️

  • Read the next page.
  • Open to the first page.
  • Look at the front cover and read the title.
  • Read from the top of the page down to the bottom.
