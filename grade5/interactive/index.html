🎮 Grade 5 — Interactive

On-screen games & explorers — tap, drag, and listen. Best on a tablet or computer.

📐 Math — 10 activities
🔍 Decimal Zoom Place tenths, zoom ×10 into hundredths, and read the number at the arrow. 🧮 Decimal Add & Subtract Coach Line up the decimal points, then add or subtract column by column with carries. 🛝 Decimal Point Slide Watch digits slide on a place-value chart when you ×/÷ by 10, 100 and 1000. 🟩 Decimal Grid Multiplier Shade rows and columns of a 10×10 grid to see tenths × tenths and whole × decimal. 🧱 Fraction Wall Shade pieces of a fraction wall to find equivalent fractions and compare sizes. 📏 Fraction Number Line Place fractions and mixed numbers on number lines and drag fractions into order. 📐 Area Explorer Resize rectangles and parallelograms on a cm grid, cut & move the triangle, then quiz. ⚙️ Function Machine Feed numbers into a secret-rule machine, study the table and name the formula. 🕵️ Counterexample Hunter Decide if a claim is always true — or break it with one counterexample. ⚫ Even/Odd Picture Proof Combine dot pictures to prove even/odd sum rules by cases, then take the quiz.

🔈 Many activities read words and questions aloud using your browser’s built-in voice — turn your sound on! These activities are for the screen and are not printable; for printable practice go back to Grade 5. ⭐ Stars you earn are saved on this device.
