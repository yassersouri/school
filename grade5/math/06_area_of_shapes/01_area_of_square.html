💡 What Is Area?
Area is the amount of space inside a flat shape. We measure area by counting how many unit squares fit inside the shape.

A unit square is a square that measures 1 unit on each side. Its area is 1 square unit (we write it as 1 unit² or, for centimeters, 1 cm²).
Counting Unit Squares Inside a Square
A 4 × 4 square holds 16 unit squares → area = 16 unit²

📏 The Formula
Counting every square would take forever for a big shape! Luckily there's a shortcut.
A square has all 4 sides equal. If one side is s, the rows make s columns of s unit squares.
Area of a square with side s Area = s × s = s²
Worked Example
Find the area of a square with side 6 cm:
Step 1: Write the formula → Area = s × s
Step 2: Replace s with 6 → 6 × 6
Step 3: Calculate → 36 cm² ✓

🔁 Going Backwards
If you know the area of a square but not the side, ask: what number times itself gives this area?
Example: Area = 25 cm² → side × side = 25 → side = 5 cm (because 5 × 5 = 25).
3 Find the Missing Side 🔍
Which number times itself gives the area?
Area = 16 cm² → side = cm
Area = 49 m² → side = m
Area = 81 cm² → side = cm
Area = 100 m² → side = m
4 Word Problems 📝
Read carefully, then use Area = s × s.
  • A square garden has sides of 8 m. What is its area? Area = m²
  • A square tile measures 20 cm on each side. What is its area? Area = cm²
  • Sara wants to cover a square floor of side 11 m with paint. How many m² of paint does she need? Area = m²
  • A square poster has an area of 64 cm². How long is one side? side = cm
