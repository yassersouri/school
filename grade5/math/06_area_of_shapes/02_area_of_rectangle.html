💡 Why length × width Works
A rectangle has two pairs of equal sides. We call the longer one length (l) and the shorter one width (w).

If you fill the rectangle with unit squares, you get w rows, each with l squares. So the total is l × w.
A 5 × 3 Rectangle
3 rows × 5 squares per row = 15 unit squares → area = 15 unit²
Area of a rectangle Area = length × width = l × w
Worked Example
Find the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 4 cm:
Step 1: Write the formula → Area = l × w
Step 2: Plug in → 8 × 4
Step 3: Calculate → 32 cm² ✓

🔁 Going Backwards
If you know the area and one side, divide!
Area = l × w  ⇒  l = Area ÷ w  and  w = Area ÷ l
Example: Area = 24 cm², length = 6 cm → width = 24 ÷ 6 = 4 cm.
2 Find the Missing Side 🔍
Use division to find the missing length or width.

3 Word Problems 📝
Decide whether to multiply or divide, then solve.
  • A classroom floor is 9 m long and 6 m wide. What is its area? Area = m²
  • A book cover is 25 cm long and 18 cm wide. How many cm² of paper covers it? Area = cm²
  • A rectangular pool has an area of 60 m² and is 10 m long. How wide is it? width = m
  • Reza is tiling a rectangular wall of 14 m by 3 m. How many m² of tiles does he need? Area = m²
  • A piece of paper has area 100 cm² and width 5 cm. How long is it? length = cm
⭐ Quick Check
A square is a special rectangle where length = width. So Area = s × s is just l × w with l = w!
