💡 The Half-Rectangle Idea
Take any triangle. If you make a copy of it, flip it upside-down, and stick the two together, you always get a rectangle (or a parallelogram).

So one triangle is exactly half the area of that rectangle!
+ copy →
Triangle area = (rectangle area) ÷ 2 = (b × h) ÷ 2
📏 Base and Height
Pick any side of the triangle as the base (b). The height (h) is the perpendicular distance from that base up to the opposite corner — straight up, not along a slanted side.

📐 Right Triangles Are Easy
In a right triangle, the two sides that meet at the right angle (the "legs") are automatically the base and the height — they're already perpendicular to each other!
3 Right Triangles 📐
