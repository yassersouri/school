💡 A Formula Is a Recipe
Think of a formula like a recipe. The letter is the ingredient (the number you put in), and the operations (×, +, −, ÷) are the cooking steps.
To use any formula: ① Read it → ② Plug in the number → ③ Calculate step by step.
📖 Formula Recipe #1: n × n (n squared)
  1. Take your number n
  2. Multiply it by itself
  3. That's your answer!
Squaring a number n × n = n²
Worked Example
Find n² when n = 4:
Step 1: Write the formula → n × n
Step 2: Replace n with 4 → 4 × 4
Step 3: Calculate → 16 ✓

📖 Formula Recipe #2: n × (n + 1) ÷ 2
  1. Take your number n
  2. Add 1 to it → that gives you n + 1
  3. Multiply n by n + 1
  4. Divide the result by 2
Adding all numbers from 1 to n 1 + 2 + 3 + ⋯ + n = n × (n + 1) ÷ 2
Worked Example
Find 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 using the formula (n = 5):
Step 1: n + 1 = 5 + 1 = 6
Step 2: n × (n + 1) = 5 × 6 = 30
Step 3: Divide by 2 → 30 ÷ 2 = 15 ✓
Check: 1+2+3+4+5 = 15 ✓ It works!
