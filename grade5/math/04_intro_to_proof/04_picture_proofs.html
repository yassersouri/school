💡 Seeing Is Believing (and Proving!)
Sometimes the best way to prove something is to draw a picture. A good picture can show you WHY a formula works — not just THAT it works. These are called visual proofs or proofs without words.
Picture Proof 1: Odd Numbers Build Perfect Squares
1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + … = a perfect square!
1 = 1²
1+3 = 2²
1+3+5 = 3²
1+3+5+7 = 4²
Each odd number (orange L-shape) wraps around the previous square to make a bigger square!
The nth odd number is 2n−1, and it adds exactly enough to go from (n−1)² to n².
1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + ⋯ + (2n−1) = n²

Picture Proof 2: The Staircase
1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = ?  Make two staircases into a rectangle!
Staircase for 1+2+3+4
→
Two copies → 4 × 5 rectangle!
The staircase has 1+2+3+4 = 10 blocks (purple).
Flip a copy (orange) and fit them together → a 4×5 = 20 rectangle.
The staircase is half of the rectangle: 20 ÷ 2 = 10 ✓
1 + 2 + 3 + ⋯ + n = n × (n + 1) ÷ 2
