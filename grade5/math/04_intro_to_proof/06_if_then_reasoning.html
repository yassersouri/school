💡 If-Then Statements
A direct proof works by connecting facts in a chain:

"IF something is true, THEN something else must also be true."

By linking several if-then statements together, you can prove things step by step — like building a bridge from what you know to what you want to show.
Example 1: If a number is divisible by 10, then it's divisible by 5

⬇️
REASON That means it equals 10 × (something). For example, 30 = 10 × 3.
⬇️
REASON But 10 = 5 × 2, so 10 × (something) = 5 × 2 × (something).

PROVED! This means the number is 5 × (something), so it's divisible by 5. ✓
Each step follows logically from the one before it. That's a proof!
Example 2: If a number is divisible by 6, then it's divisible by 3
START The number is divisible by 6.
⬇️
REASON So it equals 6 × (something).
⬇️
REASON 6 = 3 × 2, so 6 × (something) = 3 × 2 × (something).
⬇️
PROVED! The number is 3 × (something), so it's divisible by 3. ✓
