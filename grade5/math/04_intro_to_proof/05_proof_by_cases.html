💡 Check Every Case!
Sometimes a claim is about a small, finite set of things. When there are only a few cases to check, you can prove the claim by checking every single one.

This is called proof by exhaustion (or proof by cases) — you "exhaust" all the possibilities!

⚠️ This only works when the number of cases is small enough to check.
Example: Every single-digit prime > 2 is odd
Single-digit primes: 2, 3, 5, 7 — only 4 cases to check!

We didn't just try examples — we checked EVERY case. That's a proof!
