💡 What Is a Counterexample?
A counterexample is a single example that proves a claim is false.

If someone says "ALL swans are white," you don't need to check every swan in the world. You just need to find one black swan — and the claim is disproved!

In math, it works the same way. If someone claims something is ALWAYS true, finding just one case where it fails is enough to prove them wrong.
Claim: "All prime numbers are odd."
🧪 Let's check some primes:
3 is odd ✓    5 is odd ✓    7 is odd ✓    11 is odd ✓    13 is odd ✓
🔍 But wait…
2 is a prime number, and 2 is even!
❌ Counterexample: 2 — The claim is FALSE!
Claim: "The square of any number is bigger than that number."
🧪 Let's check:
3² = 9 > 3 ✓    5² = 25 > 5 ✓    10² = 100 > 10 ✓
🔍 But wait…
1² = 1, and 1 is NOT bigger than 1! Also 0² = 0.
❌ Counterexample: 1 (or 0) — The claim is FALSE!
