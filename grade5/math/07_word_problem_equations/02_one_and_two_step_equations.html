🔄 The Golden Rule: Undo It!
Every operation has an opposite ("inverse") that undoes it:
If the equation has......undo it with
+ (addition)− (subtraction)
− (subtraction)+ (addition)
× (multiplication)÷ (division)
÷ (division)× (multiplication)
Remember: do the same undo to both sides to keep the scale balanced!
Example: x + 9 = 23
The equation has "+9", so we undo it by subtracting 9 from both sides:
x + 9 − 9 = 23 − 9  →  x = 14
Check: 14 + 9 = 23 ✓

In 2 × x + 5 = 17, picture two equal boxes (each worth x) plus a little extra piece of 5, that together make 17.

First peel off the "+5" (undo it): 2 boxes = 17 − 5 = 12. Then split 12 into 2 equal boxes: each box = 12 ÷ 2 = 6.
Example: 2x + 5 = 17
Step 1 — undo the "+5" first (last operation done, undo first): 2x = 17 − 5 = 12
Step 2 — undo the "× 2": x = 12 ÷ 2 = 6
Check: 2 × 6 + 5 = 12 + 5 = 17 ✓

Word problems that need two steps 🌍

Page 4 of 4
Worked Word Problem
Story: A taxi charges $3 to start, plus $2 for every kilometer. A ride cost $17. How many kilometers was the ride?

Let x = number of kilometers.
Equation: 2x + 3 = 17
Step 1: 2x = 17 − 3 = 14   Step 2: x = 14 ÷ 2 = 7
Check: 2 × 7 + 3 = 17 ✓   The ride was 7 km.
