VERSION C — THE BIG ONE
A has 3 times of B. B has 4 times of C. C has one third of D. How many times more is A's money compared to D?
Show your work here...
A has × D's money.
BONUS — 4 LINKS!
A has 2 times as much as B. B has 3 times as much as C. C has 2 times as much as D. D has one quarter of what E has. How many times more money does A have compared to E?
Show your work here...
A has × E's money.
🎉 Well done! Even with 4 links in the chain, the method is the same: flip the fractions, multiply all the "times as many" numbers, then simplify. One long chain, one clean answer!
