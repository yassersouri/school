📚 Quick reminders:
• Two unknowns: scale one or both equations so a coefficient matches, then subtract.
• Reach zero: list every after-subtraction value down to 0, count the list, then total steps = 2×(count)−1.
• Excess/shortfall: the difference in money ÷ the difference in items = the price.
• Mystery number: x + A = k·x → x = A ÷ (k−1).
• Money chains: flip any "fraction of" clue into "times as many", then multiply the chain.
