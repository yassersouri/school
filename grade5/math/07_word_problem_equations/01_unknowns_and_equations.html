💡 An Unknown Is a Mystery Number
In a word problem, an unknown is a number we don't know yet — but we can still talk about it using a letter, like x or n. It's the number we're trying to find!

Example: "Sam has some marbles. If he gets 5 more, he'll have 12." We don't know how many marbles Sam started with — so we call it x. Once we solve the puzzle, we'll know exactly what x is.
⚖️ An Equation Is a Balance Scale
An equation is a math sentence with an equals sign (=). It says two things weigh exactly the same — like a balance scale that is perfectly level.

Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other side too — otherwise the scale tips over and it's no longer true!

🔤 Turning Phrases into Expressions
Word problems hide math inside sentences. Learn to spot the clue words:
PhraseClue wordsMath
"5 more than a number"more than → addx + 5
"7 less than a number"less than → subtractx − 7
"3 times a number"times → multiply3 × x
"a number split into 4 equal parts"split into parts → dividex ÷ 4
Watch Out: Order Matters!
"7 less than a number" means x − 7 (start with the number, take away 7) — NOT 7 − x.
A good trick: read it backwards. "7 less than a number" → "a number, less 7" → x − 7. ✓

Once you can write an expression, an equation is just two expressions joined with "=" — usually where the sentence says "is", "was", "gives", or "equals".

"8 more than a number is 15"  →  x + 8 = 15

🌍 Full Word Problems, Step by Step
For every word problem: (1) decide what the unknown is and name it x, (2) write an equation connecting x to the facts, (3) solve, (4) check your answer makes sense in the story.
Worked Example
Story: Mia had some stickers. Her friend gave her 6 more, and now she has 15. How many did Mia start with?

Step 1: Let x = stickers Mia started with.
Step 2: x + 6 = 15
Step 3: x = 15 − 6 = 9
Step 4: Check: 9 + 6 = 15 ✓   Mia started with 9 stickers.
