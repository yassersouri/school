4) Plain Sentence → Better Sentence 🌈
Add one or two adjectives to each plain sentence.
  • The dog chased the ball.
  • I found a shell at the beach.
  • We heard music in the gym.
5) Describe This Snack 🍓
Write 2–3 sentences describing your favourite snack. Use at least three adjectives.
Ex.1: fluffy, crunchy, shiny, loud.
Ex.2: sweet, muddy, gigantic, sparkly, icy.
Ex.3: Sight: bright · Sound: roaring · Touch/Taste/Smell: salty, fuzzy, stinky, smooth. (Any sensible grouping of fuzzy/smooth under touch is fine.)
Ex.4–5: Answers vary; check for clear descriptive words.
