3) Story Helpers 🧠
Use these questions to help your writing.
  • Who is in your story?
  • What do you find inside or near the tiny door?
  • What problem or surprise happens?
  • How does the story end?

4) Draft Your Story ✍️
Write a beginning, middle, and end. Use capitals, punctuation, and time-order words.
5) Check Your Work ✅
Put a check mark when you are done.
  • □ I wrote a beginning, middle, and end.
  • □ I used capital letters and punctuation.
  • □ My story makes sense from start to finish.
Open-ended sheet: There is no single correct answer. Strong stories include a clear setting, an event at the glowing door, and a simple ending.
