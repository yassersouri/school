What is the best classroom pet: fish, hamster, or rabbit? Plan your opinion, then write a short paragraph.

4) Strong Opinion Checklist ✅
Check your work when you finish.
  • □ I clearly stated my opinion.
  • □ I gave at least two reasons.
  • □ I used capitals and punctuation.
  • □ My sentences stay on the same topic.
Open-ended sheet: Accept any clear opinion paragraph with reasons. Strong answers state an opinion, include supporting details, and stay organized.
