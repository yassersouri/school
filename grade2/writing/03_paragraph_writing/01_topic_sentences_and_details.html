📌 Topic Sentences & Details

A paragraph has one big idea and supporting details.

1) Read the Model Paragraph 📖
Find the topic sentence and the supporting details.
My Garden
My garden is full of interesting things to see. Bright red tomatoes grow on the vines. Yellow flowers bring buzzing bees to the garden. I like to water the plants after school.
2) Which Sentence Is the Topic Sentence? 🎯
Circle the sentence that tells the main idea of the whole paragraph.
  • My garden is full of interesting things to see.
  • Bright red tomatoes grow on the vines.
  • Yellow flowers bring buzzing bees to the garden.
  • I like to water the plants after school.
3) Write the Topic Sentence ✍️
Read the details. Write a topic sentence that matches all of them.
  • • We read books on soft pillows.
    • The room is quiet and cozy.
    • I can choose from many stories.
