🧩 Fragments & Run-Ons

Make every sentence complete and easy to read.

1) What Is It? 🔎
Write C for complete sentence or F for fragment.
  • On the wet grass
  • The duck splashed in the pond.
  • My little brother laughed.
  • Under the table
  • The bus stopped at the corner.
2) Finish the Fragment ✍️
Add words to turn each fragment into a complete sentence.
  • The bright yellow kite
  • After school
  • My best friend and I
3) Find the Run-On 🚦
A run-on squishes two ideas together. Put a slash ( / ) where one sentence should end.
  • I opened my lunchbox it smelled like pizza.
  • The rain stopped we went outside to play.
  • My aunt waved from the porch I waved back.

4) Rewrite the Run-Ons ✂️
Turn each run-on into two clear sentences, or join ideas with a good conjunction.
  • The cat climbed the fence it looked at the birds.
  • Sam packed his skates and he went to the rink he felt excited.
  • I brushed my teeth I put on my pajamas.
