Page 1 of 2
1) Learn the Four Types 🌟
Read the chart. Then use it to help you sort the sentences below.
TypeWhat it doesExample
StatementTells somethingThe kitten is sleepy.
QuestionAsks somethingWhere is the kitten?
ExclamationShows strong feelingWhat a tiny kitten!
CommandTells someone to do somethingFeed the kitten.
2) Name the Type 🏷️

  • The class planted bean seeds.
  • What a colourful kite!
  • Put your homework in your backpack.
  • Why are the cookies still warm?
