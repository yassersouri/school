1) Two Kinds of Questions 🤔
A yes/no question gets a short answer. A tells-more question (who, what, where, when, why, how) gets a longer, more interesting answer. Write Y for yes/no or T for tells-more.
  • 1. Do you have a pet?
  • 2. What is your favorite animal and why?
  • 3. Is it sunny today?
  • 4. How do you feel about your weekend?

5) Good Discussion Checklist ✅
Use this checklist during your next class discussion.
  • ☐ I asked a question that tells more, not just yes or no.
  • ☐ I listened to the whole answer before talking.
  • ☐ I said "thank you for sharing" or something kind.
  • ☐ I took turns talking with others.
