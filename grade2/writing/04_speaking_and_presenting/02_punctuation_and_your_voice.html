❗ Punctuation & Your Voice

The end mark of a sentence changes how you say it!

Page 1 of 2
1) Read & Notice 👀
Read these three sentences out loud. Notice how your voice changes.
.  "We are going to the park." — a calm, telling voice
!  "We are going to the park!" — an excited, loud voice
?  "Are we going to the park?" — a curious voice that rises at the end
2) Add the End Mark 🖊️
Write the correct end mark ( . ! or ? ) for each sentence and feeling.
  • 1. We won the game (excited)
  • 2. What time is it (asking)
  • 3. The sun is shining today (telling)
  • 4. That is amazing (excited)
  • 5. Where did you put my shoes (asking)
3) Practice Saying It 🗣️
Read the sentences from Exercise 1 out loud, matching your voice to the end mark.
  • ☐ I used a calm voice for the period.
  • ☐ I used an excited, loud voice for the exclamation mark.
  • ☐ I made my voice rise at the end of the question.
