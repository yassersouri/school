1) Quick tutorial: borrow a ten 🔁
If the ones on top are smaller, regroup 1 ten into 10 ones.
3 tens becomes 2 tens

- 8
26
How it works:
You can't do 4 − 8.
Take 1 ten from 3 tens.
Now you have 2 tens and 14 ones.
14 − 8 = 6, and 2 tens stay 2.
