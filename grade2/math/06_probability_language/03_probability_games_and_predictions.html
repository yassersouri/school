3) Dice reasoning
Write which one is more likely.
  • Which is more likely: rolling an even number, or rolling a 6?
  • Which is more likely: rolling a number less than 3, or rolling a number greater than 3?
  • Which is more likely: rolling an odd number, or rolling a number greater than 5?
4) Button bag word problems
Read each problem and write the best answer.
  • A bag has 5 yellow buttons and 2 green buttons. Is it more likely you will pull a yellow button or a green button?
  • A jar has 4 striped marbles and 4 dotted marbles. Are you more likely to pull a striped one, a dotted one, or are they equally likely?
  • A box has 1 star sticker and 6 heart stickers. Which sticker are you more likely to pick?
Answers: 3) even number (2, 4, 6 = 3 outcomes; rolling a 6 = 1 outcome), greater than 3 ({4, 5, 6} = 3 outcomes; less than 3 {1, 2} = 2 outcomes), odd number (1, 3, 5 = 3 outcomes; greater than 5 {6} = 1 outcome)  |  4) yellow, equally likely, heart sticker
