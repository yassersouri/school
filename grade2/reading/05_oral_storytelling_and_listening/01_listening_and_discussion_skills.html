1) What Good Listeners Do 🌟
These habits help you understand and remember what someone says.
Eyes on the speaker Quiet hands and feet Listen for the whole idea Think about what you heard

  • ☐ I kept my hands and feet still and quiet.
  • ☐ I listened to the whole idea before I talked.

4) Put the Steps in Order 🔢
Number these steps 1–4 to show the order of a good conversation.
  • Take your turn to talk.
  • Listen carefully while your friend talks.
  • Listen again when it is their turn.
  • Wait until your friend is finished talking.
5) True or False? ✅
Write T for True or F for False.
  • 1. Good listeners look at their phone while someone is talking. —
  • 2. Good listeners wait for their turn to speak. —
  • 3. It's okay to interrupt if you have something to say. —
  • 4. Asking a question shows you were listening. —
Ex.4 (in order shown): 3, 1, 4, 2 — correct order is: Listen carefully → Wait until finished → Take your turn → Listen again · Ex.5: F, T, F, T.
