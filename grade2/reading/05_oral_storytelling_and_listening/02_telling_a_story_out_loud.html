1) Voice Tools 🛠️
Storytellers use these tools to keep listeners interested.
Tone — a happy, sad, or excited voice Volume — how loud or quiet you speak Pace — how fast or slow you speak Gestures — hand and face movements

4) Tell Your Story Checklist ✅
Use your plan from Page 1. Tell your story to a partner, then check off what you did.
  • ☐ I told the beginning, middle, and end in order.
  • ☐ I used a voice that matched my story.
  • ☐ I spoke loud enough for my partner to hear.
  • ☐ I looked at my partner while I talked.
