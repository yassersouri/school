🧩 Closed & Open Syllables

Closed = short vowel · Open = long vowel

Page 1 of 2
1) Learn the Two Types 📚
Read the rule boxes. Say the sample words aloud.

Closed Syllable 🔒

Ends in a consonant. The vowel is usually short.
Examples: cat, pic-nic, nap-kin.

Open Syllable 🌞

Ends in a vowel. The vowel is usually long.
Examples: go, ba-by, ti-ger.
