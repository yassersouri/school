📚 Fiction vs. Nonfiction

Stories can be made up or full of facts

Page 1 of 2
1) Learn the Difference 👀
Read the clues for each kind of text.

Fiction 🎭

  • made-up story
  • has characters
  • has a setting
  • may have a problem and solution

Nonfiction 🦉

  • gives real facts
  • teaches about a topic
  • may use headings and captions
  • often has bold words or labels
