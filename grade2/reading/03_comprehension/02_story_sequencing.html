⏰ Story Sequencing

Put the events in order: first, next, then, last

Page 1 of 2
1) Read the Story 📖
Listen for what happens first and what happens last.

On Saturday morning, Nora planted bean seeds in a small pot. Next, she poured water into the soil. Then she put the pot on a sunny windowsill. Every day she checked the pot and kept the soil damp. After one week, a tiny green sprout pushed up through the dirt. Nora smiled and drew a picture of her new plant in her science notebook.

2) Number the Events 🔢
Write 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 in the boxes.
