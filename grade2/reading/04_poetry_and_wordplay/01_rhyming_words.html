1) Read the Poem 📖
Read it out loud. Listen for the words that sound alike at the end of each line.
The Rainy Day
The rain comes down and taps the ground,
It makes a soft and gentle sound.
I watch it fall outside my door,
Then jump in puddles more and more.
2) Find the Rhyming Pairs 🔎
Circle the two words in the poem that rhyme with ground, and the two words that rhyme with door.
