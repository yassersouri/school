🎮 Grade 2 — Interactive

On-screen games & explorers — tap, drag, and listen. Best on a tablet or computer.

🧮 Math — 11 activities
🧱 Place Value Builder Build numbers to 1000 with hundreds, tens and ones blocks — then break and group them. 🧩 Expanded Form Match Match 347 to 300 + 40 + 7 and back again — zero heroes too! 📍 Number Line Drop Drag a pin to where a number lives on a 0–100 or 0–1000 line. ➕ Column Addition Coach Add 2-digit numbers step by step: ones, carry the ten, then tens. ➖ Subtraction Borrow Coach Trade a ten for 10 ones with blocks, take away, and write the answer. 🪙 Coin Counter Count Canadian coins and tap coins from a purse to make an amount. 🏪 Corner Store Pay the exact price with coins and work out change from $1. 📏 Ruler Lab Estimate, then measure objects in cm — and choose cm or m. 🎡 Probability Spinner Design a spinner, predict, spin 50 times, and use chance words. 🔍 Shape Detective Count sides, corners, faces and edges; sort shapes; spot 3-D objects around us. 🍎 Array Builder (Bonus: Grade 3 preview) Build rows of things and see repeated addition become multiplication.
📖 Reading — 9 activities
✂️ Syllable Splitter Tap between letters to split words into syllables, then hear each part. 🗂️ Syllable Type Sort Sort syllables into closed, open, silent-e, vowel team and bossy-R bins. 🧪 Prefix & Suffix Lab Add un-, re-, -ful, -less and more to build words that match a meaning. 🐝 Spelling Bee Hear a word and a sentence, type the spelling, and see which letters are right. 🧵 Story Sequencer Read a short story, then put its events in order: first, next, then, last. 📖 Fiction or Nonfiction? Decide if a passage is made-up or true, and pick a paragraph's main idea. 🕹️ Rhyme & Alliteration Arcade Find rhymes, tongue-twister sounds and sound words like buzz and splash. 🌈 Simile Maker Finish “as … as” and “like a” similes, then build your own silly ones. 👂 Listen & Answer Hear a passage read with word highlighting, then answer questions about it.

🔈 Many activities read words and questions aloud using your browser’s built-in voice — turn your sound on! These activities are for the screen and are not printable; for printable practice go back to Grade 2. ⭐ Stars you earn are saved on this device.
